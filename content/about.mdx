## Background

I'm an Enterprise AI, Cloud, and DevSecOps Architect with over a decade of experience
operating large-scale infrastructure for banks, government agencies, and enterprise
clients across Morocco and France. My work sits at the intersection of three
disciplines: **cloud-native platform engineering**, **production AI/GenAI deployment**,
and **security-first automation**.

## How I work

I lead with observability. Before shipping any platform — whether it's a Kubernetes
cluster, a GenAI orchestration layer, or a CI/CD pipeline — I make sure it can be seen,
measured, and trusted in production. That philosophy runs through my work with
Grafana, ELK, Dynatrace, and Prometheus, and it's why security gates get built into
the pipeline itself rather than bolted on afterward.

## What I'm building toward

My PhD research focused on multi-cloud and hybrid-cloud architectures — workload
orchestration, disaster recovery, active-active deployments, and near-zero-downtime
systems. That research now shows up directly in how I architect production AI
infrastructure: systems designed to fail gracefully, recover fast, and stay
observable under load.
